Put your shop photos in this folder, using these exact file names:

  shop-1.jpg   -> shows in the "Shop front" gallery card
  shop-2.jpg   -> shows in the "Frames wall" gallery card
  shop-3.jpg   -> shows in the "Eye-test corner" gallery card

You can rename the files to whatever you like, or add more photos —
just update the matching "src" values inside the SHOP.gallery list
near the bottom of the <script> in index.html.

Until a photo is added, that gallery card shows a dashed "add your
photo" placeholder instead of a broken image, so the page still
looks tidy.
